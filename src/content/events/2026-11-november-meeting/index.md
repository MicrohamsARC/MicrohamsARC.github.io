## Presentation: Beyond the Battery Label: Understanding Real-World Battery Performance

Note: this meeting will be online only.

Frustrated with seemingly incongruous results from the use of portable power tool batteries for powering amateur radio products, Scott set out to understand why he was seeing battery performance issues, and understand what was the REAL situation with OEM and aftermarket power tool batteries.  Armed with a West Mountain Computerized Battery Analyzer and a plan, Scott dove into the murky world of power tool batteries and Tuesday he'll tell us how he survived the ordeal and what he learned!
